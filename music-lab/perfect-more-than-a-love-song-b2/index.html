MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

What Does “Perfect” Mean?

Discuss love, memories and expectations—without needing to share anything personal.

Conversation starter

Teacher follow-ups: Why? Can you give an example? Would everyone agree? Has your opinion changed?

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

Seven Words Behind the Feeling

Reveal each word, say the example aloud, then answer the personal question.

MISSION 03 · LISTEN & NOTICE

Perfect — Read Between the Lines 🎧

Listen to Ed Sheeran's “Perfect” using the official video or your preferred licensed music service.

Find the official music video ↗

Short lyric spotlight

“I found a love for me”

What does the narrator's choice of found suggest about the story? What might have happened before this moment?

Listening checkpoints

Opening: What do we learn about the relationship's beginning? Middle: Which shared moments and visual images make the relationship feel intimate? Ending: What future does the narrator imagine?

Lyric detective (use your own lyric sheet)

Find one line referring to a past memory, one that describes a present feeling, and one suggesting a future hope. Read or paraphrase each line, then explain its meaning.

MISSION 04 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Past Memories, Present Feelings, Future Dreams

PurposeLanguage
Finished memoryPast Simple: We met at a party.
Experience connected to nowPresent Perfect: We've known each other for years.
Future hopeWill / hope to: I hope we'll stay close.
Nuanced opinionI see your point, but… / To some extent…

Speaking tip: Don't only describe the song—connect the grammar to a real or imaginary relationship.

Teacher challenge: Transform one correct quiz answer into a personal example.

MISSION 05 · MAIN INTERACTIVE CHALLENGE

Love Under the Microscope 🔍

Take a position, explain your reasoning, and respond to a counterargument.

MISSION 06 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

Two Friends, Two Perspectives

Discuss whether the song presents an idealized version of love. Each response changes the reaction.

Speaking upgrade: Give your own response instead of choosing one of the options.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

Ten Years Later: Rewrite the Story ✍️

Imagine the couple ten years after the song. They may still be together—or their lives may have changed. Create a believable continuation, not a perfect fairy tale.

Present your sequel: Speak for 90 seconds, then answer: Is their relationship still “perfect”? Why or why not?

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

Is Perfect Love a Myth? 🎤

“A meaningful relationship doesn't need to be perfect.”

Give a two-minute response: state your opinion, give two reasons, refer to the song, consider an opposing view, and finish with a conclusion.
