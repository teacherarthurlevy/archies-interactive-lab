Mission 05 · Language Focus

Relative Clauses & Character Adjectives

Use who, whose, where, which, and that to describe people and scenes.

Notice the grammar

Who: Lara is the woman who loves books.
Whose: Daniel is the man whose job offer creates tension.
Where: The café where they met is crowded.
Which/that: The message that Lara misunderstood caused a problem.
Non-defining: Lara, who is guarded, rarely opens up.

Use commas around non-essential information. Don't use that in non-defining relative clauses.
