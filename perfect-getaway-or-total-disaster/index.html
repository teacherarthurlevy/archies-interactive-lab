Teacher's guide — pacing and objectives

Suggested 75–90 minutes: Warm-up 8, Escape 10, Vocabulary 10, Budget 12, Disasters 15, Debate 15, Podcast 12, Final 8. For a shorter class, skip cards or scenarios freely without XP penalties.

Objectives: discuss travel preferences, negotiate priorities, justify a budget, use travel vocabulary, make conditional suggestions, handle travel problems, argue respectfully, and narrate a story. Encourage follow-up questions and extended speaking. Budget figures are fictional. No audio functionality is included.
