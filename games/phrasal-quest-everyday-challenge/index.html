How to play

Level 1 — Decode the Scene: identify the phrasal verb from a picture and sentence. Level 2 — Choose the Reply: select natural everyday responses. Level 3 — Complete the Story: use the expressions in context.

You start with three hearts. Wrong answers cost one heart, but the game explains the correct answer. When your hearts run out, you can restart the current level. Each correct answer earns 100 points; completing a level earns a 150-point bonus. The final challenge is a speaking quest.
