03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

The Time-Travel Grammar Machine ⏳

How reported speech works

Direct: “I am tired.” → Reported: She said (that) she was tired.

Direct speechReported speech
Present SimplePast Simple
Present ContinuousPast Continuous
Present PerfectPast Perfect
Past SimplePast Perfect
willwould
am/is/are going towas/were going to

Extension: Present Perfect Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous.

Say: She said (that) she was busy. Tell: She told me (that) she was busy.

Time & place: now → then; today → that day; tomorrow → the next day; yesterday → the day before; here → there; this → that.

These are the standard backshift patterns in the worksheet. In real speech, backshift and time-word changes depend on context and are not always necessary.

Quick check · immediate feedback and retries

Speaking upgrade: Explain why you changed each verb.

06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

The Newsroom Interview 🎤

Round 1: Teacher is a celebrity who shares three pieces of news. Student is a journalist who interviews them, then reports the news.

Teacher's secret statements:

“I'm working on a new project.” · “I have visited three countries this year.” · “I will announce something tomorrow.”

Student's task: Ask follow-up questions, then report all three statements to a different person.

Round 2: Swap roles. Student invents three statements (past, present, future). Teacher reports them — student checks the grammar.

Round 3: Report a real conversation from your week. Use said, told, and at least two time expressions.

07 · CREATE & APPLY

The Message That Got Mixed Up ✍️

Your friend missed three messages. Create a short, accurate update using reported speech.

Original messages

1. Sofia: “I'm preparing a surprise party today.”

2. Lucas: “I bought the decorations yesterday.”

3. Emma: “I'll bring the cake tomorrow.”

Challenge: Imagine you are reporting the messages the following day. Change the verbs, pronouns, and time expressions appropriately.

Speak: Present your report without reading every word. Then explain one tense change.

08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

Can You Keep the Story Straight? 🏆

Final speaking mission

Your teacher tells you four short facts: one in Present Simple, one in Present Perfect, one in Past Simple, and one using will. Report all four accurately, including at least one time change.

Bonus: Report “I have been studying English for two years” using Past Perfect Continuous.
