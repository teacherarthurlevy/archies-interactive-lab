MISSION 03 · GRAMMAR FOCUS

Rules + Exam Questions 📚

Past Simple

Finished past action: I visited her yesterday. I didn't visit her. Did you visit her?

yesterday · last week · ago · in 2020
Present Perfect Simple

Experience or connection to now: I have visited Rio. She hasn't finished. Have you ever been there?

ever · never · already · yet · just · for · since
May / Might for the future

Both show possibility: I may go. She might stay. He may not come.

may / might + base verb (no “to”)
Look like / Be like

What does she look like? → appearance: tall, brown eyes.

What is she like? → personality: friendly, patient.

Past Simple vs. Present Perfect

I saw that movie yesterday (finished time). I've seen that movie (life experience). Have you ever broken your arm? — Yes, I have. I broke it when I was ten.
