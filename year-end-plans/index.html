Mission 01 · Understand

December Check-In

Start with your own plans. Answer in detail and ask your partner a follow-up question.

PT-BR: Responda em inglês com detalhes. Dê motivos, exemplos e faça uma pergunta de volta.

Keep the conversation going:

“What about you?” · “That reminds me of…” · “I'd rather… because…” · “If everything goes according to plan…”

Mission 02 · Notice

Holiday Vocabulary Flip Cards

Guess the meaning, reveal the card, and use the expression in your own holiday sentence.

PT-BR: Revele a tradução e o exemplo. Depois crie uma frase própria.

Speaking challenge: Use at least four expressions to describe your ideal holiday break.
Mission 03 · Practice

Plans, Predictions & Preferences

Choose the best English expression and discover why it works.

PT-BR: Faça o quiz e explique oralmente o motivo de cada resposta.
Language Detective:

be going to = intentions and evidence-based predictions · present continuous = arranged future plans · might / may = possibilities · would rather = preferences · if + present, will = likely future results.

Mission 04 · Debate

The Holiday Hot-Take Arena

Take a position, build your argument, and respond to the opposite opinion.

PT-BR: Escolha um lado, apresente dois argumentos, dê um exemplo e rebata um contra-argumento. Não existem respostas certas para opiniões.

THE MOTION

Choose your position.

Build your case

Use: “First of all…” · “Another reason is…” · “For example…” · “However…” · “Ultimately…”

Bonus: Switch sides. Defend the opinion you personally disagree with for 45 seconds.
Mission 05 · Decide

Holiday Dilemma Simulator

Unexpected plans, conflicting priorities, and difficult decisions. What would you do?

PT-BR: Leia a situação, escolha uma decisão e justifique em inglês. Revele uma complicação para adaptar o plano.

THE SITUATION

What's your call?

Speaking task: Explain your decision, the trade-off, and how you would communicate it to the people involved.

Mission 08 · Review

The Final Year-End Challenge

Put everything together in one extended conversation.

PT-BR: Converse por 3–4 minutos, sem ler um roteiro. Use vocabulário novo, expresse opinião e reaja a um imprevisto.
FINAL QUEST · 3–4 MINUTES

Your Holidays, Your Rules?

Tell us your Christmas and New Year's plans. Compare your ideal holiday with what you will probably do. Explain your preferences, budget, and travel possibilities. Then defend one controversial holiday opinion.

Teacher's guide — Suggested class flow

Warm-up 8 min · Vocabulary 10 min · Language 10 min · Debate 18 min · Dilemmas 12 min · Branching conversation 10 min · Holiday plan 10 min · Final quest 10 min. For a 60-minute class, select fewer debates and dilemmas.

Keep speaking time high: ask “Why?”, “What makes you say that?”, “Can you give an example?”, and “What would change your mind?” Encourage respectful disagreement, conditionals, future forms, and extended answers. Christmas activities may differ across cultures and personal beliefs; students can discuss other year-end traditions.
