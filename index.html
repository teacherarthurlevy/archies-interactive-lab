Mission 06 · Speak

Constructive Feedback Clinic

Turn blunt feedback into language that is specific, respectful, and actionable.

PT-BR: Reescreva cada frase usando a estrutura Situation → Behavior → Impact → Next Step. Revele um modelo para comparar.
Feedback framework

Situation: When did it happen? Behavior: What happened, objectively? Impact: Why did it matter? Next step: What needs to change?

Mission 07 · Lead

Build the Follow-Up Plan

A good one-on-one ends with a clear agreement—not just a good conversation.

PT-BR: Escolha um caso da missão 4 e construa um plano concreto. As anotações ficam salvas neste navegador.
Reveal a strong follow-up example

“Let's agree on two actions. You'll identify the blocked tasks by tomorrow afternoon, and I'll speak with the product owner about priorities. We'll check progress on Thursday and adjust the release scope if necessary.”

Suggested live lesson: Warm-up 8 min · Vocabulary 10 min · Language Detective 8 min · Case Room 25 min · Branching conversation 10 min · Feedback Clinic 10 min · Action Plan 8 min · Final Quest 8 min. Select fewer cases for a 60-minute class.

Role-play: Teacher plays the employee and challenges the manager's first response with “What if…?”, “How would that help?”, and “When should we review this?”. Prioritize fluency, empathy, clarity, and ownership.

Scoring: XP measures mission completion, not professional management competence. Scenarios are fictional practice, not HR/legal guidance.
