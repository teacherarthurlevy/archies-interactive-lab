Mission 03 · Language Focus

Tone Changes Everything

Practice I-statements, tactful questions, polite boundaries, and regret.

Notice the language

Clarification: “Could you tell me what you meant?”
I-statement: “I felt left out when I saw the photos.”
Boundary: “I can't reply during meetings, but I'll get back to you.”
Repair: “I'm sorry. That came across as harsher than I intended.”
Regret: “I wish I'd asked before assuming.”

Make it sound better

Rewrite these messages to make them clearer and more respectful:

1. “Why are you ignoring me?”
2. “Your idea is bad.”
3. “Stop texting me.”

Teacher guide · 75–95 minutes

Warm-up 7 min, vocabulary 10, language 12, inbox 22, branching 12, role-play 12, creation 12, final 8. For B1, allow sentence frames and short replies. For B2, demand nuanced tone explanations, alternatives, and follow-up questions. The simulator's relationship meters are fictional teaching devices; there are no objectively perfect social responses. The teacher can freely navigate and skip without awarding XP. No audio or recording.
