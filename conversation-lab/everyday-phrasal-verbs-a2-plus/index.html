Mission 03 · Language focus

Make It Sound Natural 🧩

Notice how these expressions work in real sentences. Select an answer and read the immediate explanation. Retry whenever you like.

Useful language patterns

Run into someone: “I ran into an old friend.”
Put something off: “I put the meeting off.” / “I put it off.”
Figure something out: “We figured it out.”
Bring something up: “She brought it up.”
Turn something down: “He turned the offer down.”
Catch up with someone: “Let's catch up soon.”
Come across something: “I came across an old photo.”

Notice: with a pronoun, say “put it off,” not “put off it.” Many of these verbs have irregular past forms: ran, brought, came.
