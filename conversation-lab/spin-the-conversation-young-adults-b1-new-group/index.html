Mission 03 · Practice

Language Focus

Use natural B1 expressions to give opinions, disagree politely, and make suggestions.

Giving an opinion

“In my opinion…”
“The main reason is…”

Polite disagreement

“I see your point, but…”
“I'm not sure I agree because…”

Suggesting

“What if we…?”
“We could try…”

Comparing options

“On the one hand…”
“The advantage is…”

Choose the most natural response

Try again when necessary; feedback explains each answer.

Mission 06 · Collaborate

Speak & Solve

Choose roles and negotiate a decision together. Both speakers must explain and respond.

Roommates

One wants a quiet home; the other invites friends often. Agree on three rules.

Weekend planners

One wants to save money; the other wants an exciting experience. Plan a Saturday.

Work teammates

A deadline is close and tasks are unequal. Divide the work fairly.

Friends with a problem

A friend shared private information. Repair trust and set a boundary.

Use at least three: “I understand, but…” · “What if we…?” · “The problem with that is…” · “Could we agree on…?”
Mission 07 · Create

Create & Apply

Create a practical “Better Decisions Guide” for young adults. Write three tips, one example, and one useful phrase.

Present your guide to a partner. Ask: “Which tip would you actually use?”

Mission 08 · Reflect

Final Quest & Reflection

Final speaking challenge: You have 90 seconds to explain a difficult choice you made or might make. Describe your options, compare advantages and disadvantages, explain your decision, and answer one challenging follow-up.
