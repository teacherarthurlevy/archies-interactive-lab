01 · Warm-Up & Engagement

Hello! Nice to Meet You 👋

Today is about you. There are no perfect answers. Speak, ask questions and have fun!

👋 The basics

What's your name? Where are you from? Where do you live? What do you do?

Try: “My name is… I'm from… I'm a…”

🎧 Your favorites

What's your favorite food, movie, music or game? Why do you like it?

Try: “My favorite… is… because…”

☀️ Your day

What time do you wake up? Do you work or study? What do you do in the evening?

Try: “I usually… I sometimes…”

🌎 Your goals

Why are you learning English? When do you want to use English?

Try: “I want to speak English because…”

Teacher observation: Start naturally. Notice comprehension, willingness to speak, sentence length, use of am/is/are, and ability to ask a question. Avoid correcting every error.

One more question…

Ask your teacher two questions about their life.

02 · Vocabulary Discovery

Who Are You? What Do You Do?

Explore personality adjectives and action verbs. Reveal the meaning and Portuguese translation. Say a true sentence with each word.

Talk: Choose three adjectives that describe you. Choose three action verbs you use in your everyday life. Say why.
03 · Language Focus

Be, Do & Like

👤 Verb to be · Who you are

Positive: I'm friendly. She's funny. We're students.

Negative: I'm not shy. He isn't lazy.

Question: Are you outgoing? Is she patient?

🏃 Action verbs · What you do

I work. I study. I cook dinner.

He works. She studies.

Question: Do you cook? Does he study?

Negative: I don't work on Sundays.

❤️ Likes & dislikes

I like music. I like watching movies.

I don't like waking up early.

Do you like traveling?

💬 Keep talking

“I'm creative because I like drawing.”

“I usually study after work.”

“What about you?”

Notice: “I am happy” = a state or trait. “I play games” = an action or routine. Avoid “I am play games.”

04 · Main Interactive Challenge

Spin the Conversation 🎲

Tap the button to get a random conversation question. Answer in a full sentence and add a reason or example. Your teacher asks a follow-up.

Conversation prompt

Ready for a question?

Click “New question” to start.

A1 mode

Answer with 1–3 sentences. Use “I'm…”, “I like…”, or “I usually…”

A2 mode

Answer with 3–5 sentences, add “because,” and ask a follow-up question.

Teacher: Encourage authentic answers. Follow up with “Why?”, “How often?”, “Who with?” and “Can you tell me more?”
05 · Branching Conversation

What Would You Say? 💬

Choose the most natural reply. See the consequence, then practice the follow-up aloud.

06 · Communicative Practice

Let's Have a Real Conversation ☕

Choose a scenario. Student and teacher take turns. Switch roles after each round.

☕ At a coffee shop

Meet someone new. Ask their name, where they're from, and what they like doing.

🎉 At a friend's party

Introduce yourself and describe your personality and interests.

📱 Talking about weekends

Discuss your normal weekend, your favorite activities and your plans.

🌍 Making a new international friend

Talk about your city, your hobbies and why you want to improve your English.

Speaking challenge: Ask at least four questions, use three personality adjectives and four action verbs, and react naturally with “Really?”, “Me too!”, or “That's interesting!”
07 · Create & Apply

My Personal Profile ✍️

Write a friendly introduction for a new classmate. Use true information.

Speaking task: Close your notes and introduce yourself in 45 seconds. Your teacher asks three follow-up questions.
08 · Final Quest & Reflection

Our First Real Chat 🎯

Finish with a friendly conversation. Your teacher listens and makes a provisional A1–A2 assessment.

Final speaking prompts

  1. Tell me about yourself.
  2. How would your friends describe you?
  3. What do you usually do on weekdays?
  4. What do you like doing in your free time?
  5. Why do you want to learn English?

A2 extension: Explain your answers, ask three questions, and talk about a recent experience using any English you know.
