LET'S GO SHOPPING!

Can you find the perfect outfit for a party without spending more than $100? Visit different stores, talk to shop assistants, compare prices and make smart choices.

01 · Warm-up

Outfit check

Shopping habits

Do you prefer shopping online or in a store? Why?

Party plans

Budget decision

Useful sentence starters

Quick speaking challenge: Describe two outfits and choose your favorite.

02 · Vocabulary discovery

Six Words. Then We Speak.

Guess the meaning, reveal the Portuguese translation and example, then move on. Exactly six cards.

Shopping vocabulary

Use all six words in a conversation

Shop assistant: Hello! Can I help you?

Customer: Yes, I'm looking for an outfit. Do you have this jacket in my size?

Shop assistant: Of course. What size do you need?

Customer: Medium, please. Can I try it on? Is there a discount?

Shop assistant: Yes! The price is $35, but there's a 10% discount.

Customer: Great! It fits. I'll take it.

Role-play: Change the item, size, price and discount. Swap roles and repeat without reading.

03 · Language focus

Shopping English That Works

This / That

This shirt (near me). That shirt (far away).

These / Those

These shoes (near me). Those shoes (far away).

Polite requests

I'd like a blue jacket, please. Can I try it on?

Prices & sizes

How much is it? It's $24. How much are they? They're $40.

Speaking upgrade

Say each answer again, but more politely. Example: “Blue jacket.” → “I'd like to try on that blue jacket, please.”

04 · Main challenge

The $100 Party Outfit Challenge

Shop in three different stores. Choose one top, one bottom and one pair of shoes. Optional accessory. Your total must be $100 or less.

Explain your choices aloud

“I'd like this ... because ... . It costs ... . My total is ... . I have ... left.”

05 · Branching conversation

Three Stores, Three Conversations

Choose the best response. A different shop assistant reacts to your decision.

06 · Communicative practice

Shop Like a Pro

Store 1 · Fashion boutique

Ask for a jacket in a different size and color. Ask to try it on. Decide whether to buy it.

Store 2 · Sneaker shop

Compare two pairs of shoes. Ask about prices. Choose one and explain why.

Store 3 · Accessories kiosk

Ask about a discount. Explain your budget and negotiate by choosing a cheaper item.

Surprise problem!

Your favorite item is sold out in your size. Ask for an alternative and make a new decision.

Teacher follow-ups: Why do you prefer those? How much money do you have left? Would you buy this or that?

07 · Create & apply

My Shopping Plan

Write a short shopping plan (5–7 sentences) using your outfit choices, prices, this/that/these/those, and “I'd like.” Then present it as a mini fashion pitch.

Creative twist

Your friend has only $60. Recommend a cheaper outfit and explain what to change.

08 · Final quest

Checkout Challenge

Complete the final questions, then role-play the full shopping experience without reading.
