Mission 03 · Notice & Practice

Grammar Power-Up

Present continuous

am/is/are + verb-ing

She is playing the flute. They are drawing.

Possessive adjectives

Before a noun: my, your, his, her, its, our, their.

This is her book.

Possessive pronouns

Replace the noun: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.

The book is hers.

Possessive 's

One owner: John's car. Regular plural: my parents' car. Irregular plural: children's shoes.

Infinitive of purpose

to + base verb explains WHY.

We went to the lake to swim.

Five senses

see / sight; hear / hearing; smell; taste; touch / feel.

Mission 05 · Make Decisions

Branching Conversation: The School Trip

You and your friend are planning a visit to the countryside. Choose a response and see what happens.

Mission 06 · Speak

Communicative Practice

Role-play A: Lost backpack

Student A: Your backpack is missing. Describe it and explain how you feel. Student B: Ask whose backpack it is and where it was last seen.

Role-play B: Nature trip

Plan a picnic near a river. Say what you are taking, why you need it, and what you can see, hear, smell and touch.

Role-play C: At home

Describe what five people are doing. Use present continuous and at least three possessives.

Quick debate

Which is better: the city or the countryside? Use three nature words and two opposite adjectives.

Challenge

Speak for 60 seconds about a day in the countryside. Your teacher can ask “Why?”, “Whose?” and “What are they doing?”

Mission 07 · Create & Apply

My Perfect Countryside Day

Create a short paragraph (6–9 sentences). Include: a place, two feelings, two senses, three present-continuous verbs, two possessives, an infinitive of purpose and one opposite pair.

Model: We are visiting a village to see a waterfall. I'm happy because the place is beautiful. My sister is taking pictures with her camera. The camera is hers. I can hear the river. The lake is big, but the pond is small.
