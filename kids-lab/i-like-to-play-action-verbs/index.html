Teacher guide · 30–45 minutes

For a five-year-old, prioritize speaking and physical movement. Suggested timing: hello 3 min, cards 7, like to 5, picture game 6, movement 5, happy day 6, conversation 5, final 4. Use picture-first teaching and one instruction at a time. The teacher reads all prompts. English targets: play, run, jump, draw, dance, sing, read, watch cartoons, go to school, ride a bike, eat, drink; I like to + verb. For food continuity: I like to eat apples / drink milk. No audio or recording. Teacher may skip missions without awarding XP.
