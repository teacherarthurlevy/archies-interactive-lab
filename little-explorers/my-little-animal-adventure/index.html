My Little Animal Adventure! 🐾

Look. Say. Move. Play!
ANYBODY CAN SPEAK ENGLISH, YOU JUST DON'T KNOW YET.
Learning Lab 2.0

01 · HELLO · 3 MIN

Hello, animal explorer!

🧸🐾

Hello, Teddy!

Teacher: wave, ask “How are you?” Then hide a toy and ask “Where's Teddy?” Review “in the box” or “on the chair.” Ask the child to jump once and say “I like to jump.”
02 · DISCOVER · 5 MIN

Meet six animals

Teacher: point, model the animal name, invite one repetition and a gesture.

03 · TALK · 4 MIN

What is it? Can it...?

Teacher plays Teddy. Ask the questions aloud; let the child answer with a word first, then model the short sentence. Practice all six animals if time allows.

04 · PLAY · 4 MIN

Find the animal!

Teacher reads the clue. Child points or taps and names the animal. This game is independent of the video.

05 · WATCH · 4 MIN

Movie time! 🎬

🐦🐘🐟

Yes, I Can! — Super Simple Songs

Watch together and enjoy the animals and actions. No quiz. No questions to answer. Moving or singing along is optional.

▶ Watch on YouTube

Teacher: open the video, supervise playback, then return to the lesson. The song is about animals and what they can do; some animals differ from our six cards.

06 · MOVE · 4 MIN

Animal action game!

Teacher: ask “Can you...?” Child says “Yes, I can!” and acts it out. For fly/swim/climb use pretend movements only; stay on the floor.

07 · CREATE · 3 MIN

My favorite animal

Choose one, then tell Teddy about it!

I like cats!

A cat can jump.

08 · GOODBYE · 3 MIN

Super explorer! ⭐

🏆🐾

Choose any animal. Name it. Say one thing it can do. Show one action. Say goodbye to Teddy!

How was today?

Teacher guide · 30 minutes

3 + 5 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 3 + 3 = 30 minutes. Read everything aloud. Use gestures, toys and short sentences. Accept pointing or one-word answers and model full sentences. Skip freely without XP. Video is for enjoyment only. No letter writing required.
