MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Catch Up! ☕

Imagine we haven't talked in a month. Have a natural conversation. Ask follow-up questions.

What's something good that happened this week?
What have you been busy with lately?
What are you looking forward to?

Teacher: aim for 3–5 minutes of uninterrupted conversation. Ask “How come?” and “What happened next?”

MISSION 02 · SIX USEFUL EXPRESSIONS

Six Small Cards 🃏

Tap each compact card to reveal its meaning, Portuguese translation, and example. Practice one sentence with each expression.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Keep the Conversation Going 💬

React naturally: “No way!” · “That sounds great!” · “Oh, really?”

Ask for details: “How did that go?” · “What was that like?”

Share your experience: “That reminds me of...” · “Something similar happened to me.”

Keep it flowing: “Speaking of which...” · “By the way...”

MISSION 04 · MAIN GAME

The Everyday Roulette 🎡

Spin to get a casual conversation topic. Each topic has four different questions. Speak for 60–90 seconds, then use a follow-up to keep the exchange natural.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CHAT

Plans Changed! 📱

A friend cancels your weekend plans at the last minute. How do you keep the conversation friendly?

Teacher: ask the student to say the selected response aloud and suggest an alternative.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Conversation Switch-Up 🔄

Choose a scenario. Talk for two minutes, then switch roles.

Old friends

Meet after a long time. Catch up on life, work, and hobbies.

New classmates

Find three things you have in common without using yes/no questions only.

Weekend planning

One person wants adventure; the other wants to relax. Make a plan.

Speaking challenge: Ask at least three genuine follow-up questions and react naturally to the answers.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Conversation Starter Pack ✍️

Write three questions you'd actually ask someone new. Avoid questions that only need “yes” or “no.”

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

The Natural Conversation Challenge 🏆

Talk for three minutes!

1. Start with a genuine question.

2. Share a personal experience.

3. React to your partner's answer.

4. Ask two follow-up questions.

5. Change topics naturally with “Speaking of which...”
