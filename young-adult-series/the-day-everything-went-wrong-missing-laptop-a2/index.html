MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Have You Ever Had a Terrible Day? 😅

Tell a short story. It can be real or imaginary.

Sentence starters: Yesterday, I... / Last weekend, I... / First... Then... Finally...

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

Words That Make Stories Better 📚

Reveal each word, say the example, and create your own sentence.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Past Simple: What Happened?

Regular verbs

arrive → arrived · check → checked · move → moved · notice → noticed

Irregular verbs

go → went · bring → brought · find → found · see → saw · have → had · get → got

Negative and questions

I didn't find my phone. · Did you see my bag? · What did you do?

Sequence your story

First, I left home. Then, I missed the bus. Suddenly, it rained. Luckily, my friend helped. Finally, I arrived.

MISSION 04 · STORY DETECTIVE

CASE FILE 07: The Missing Laptop 💻

Fictional teaching case: This scenario is realistic but is not a documented real-world incident.

A realistic fictional case inspired by everyday mistakes. A laptop disappeared from a coworking café. Read five pieces of evidence, separate facts from guesses, and reconstruct the timeline. Nobody should be accused without evidence.

Detective tip: Say what the clue proves, what it does not prove, and use a Past Simple verb.
MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

At the Café: Ask for Help 💬

Choose how to speak with the café employee. Be clear, polite, and specific.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Real-Life Decisions 🎭

Choose the best next action and explain it in English. Then retell what happened using the Past Simple.

Role-play: The Witness

Teacher = café employee or reporter. Student = Alex or witness. Answer: Where were you? What did you see? What can you confirm? What is only a guess? How did it end? Swap roles.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

Write a Realistic Incident Report ✍️

Write a clear 70–100-word incident report about the café case or a similar fictional event. Explain what you know, what you don't know, and what happened next.

Speaking extension: Present the report without reading. Your teacher will ask three follow-up questions.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

The 90-Second Case Briefing 🏆

Present the missing laptop case. What happened? Which facts are confirmed? What was only a guess?

Include four Past Simple verbs, three sequencing words, two pieces of evidence, and one conclusion. Answer two questions from your teacher.
