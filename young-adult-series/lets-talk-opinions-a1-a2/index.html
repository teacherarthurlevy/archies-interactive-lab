MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

What Do You Think? 💬

Choose a question. Give your opinion and ask your partner a question. There are no right or wrong answers.

Speak like this: “I think...” → “Because...” → “What about you?”

Example: I think weekends are great because I can relax. What about you?

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

Words for Opinions 🗣️

Guess the meaning. Reveal the Portuguese translation and English example. Say your own sentence. You can skip any card or this section.

Try it: “I prefer ___ because ___.” Then ask: “What about you?”
MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Simple Ways to Share Opinions 🧠

I think / I don't think

I think friends are important.
I don't think money is everything.

Like / Prefer

I like going out.
I prefer texting to calling.

Because / But

I like my city because it's fun.
I agree, but I prefer staying home.

Agree / Disagree

I agree with you!
I don't agree because...
What do you think?

Extra A2 challenge: “Friends should be honest.” “Calling is better than texting.” Conversation tip: Give a reason and ask another question.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Friends & Everyday Life 🎭

Speak with your teacher or a partner. Change roles after 2 minutes. Use short sentences, but always ask a follow-up question.

Round 1: “Texting is better than calling.”

Round 2: “Good friends should talk every day.”

Round 3: “Staying home is better than going out.”

Round 4: “Couples can have different hobbies.”

Challenge: Say your opinion + because + one question. Try: “Really?” “Why?” “What about you?”

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Opinion Post ✍️

Write a short, friendly opinion post about friends, relationships or everyday life. Use the sentence starters.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

The 2-Minute Opinion Challenge 🏆

Let's talk about friendship!

Final opinion: “Good friends don't need to talk every day.”

Prepare for 30 seconds. Then speak for two minutes with your teacher. Say if you agree or disagree, give two reasons, and ask two questions.

Useful phrases: I think... / I agree because... / I don't agree because... / What about you? / Why?

A2 bonus: Use should or better than.
