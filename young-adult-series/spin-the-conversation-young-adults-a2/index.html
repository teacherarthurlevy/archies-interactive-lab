MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Catch Up! ☕

Talk about your day and your free time. Answer in two or three sentences. Ask your partner a question, too.

What do you usually do after work or class?
What did you do last weekend?
What are you going to do next weekend?

Teacher: model short answers with because. Encourage one follow-up: Why? Who with? Where? When?

MISSION 02 · SIX USEFUL EXPRESSIONS

Six Useful Words 🃏

Tap a small card to see the meaning, Portuguese translation, and example. Make one short sentence.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Keep Talking! 💬

React naturally: “Really?” · “That's nice!” · “Wow!”

Ask for details: “Why?” · “Who with?” · “How was it?”

Share your experience: “Me too!” · “I like that too.” · “I don't usually do that.”

Keep it flowing: “What about you?” · “And you?” · “By the way...”

MISSION 04 · MAIN GAME

The Everyday Roulette 🎡

Spin to get a casual conversation topic. Each topic has four different questions. Speak for 30–60 seconds. Give one reason or example. Ask your partner one question. You can choose a topic without spinning.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CHAT

Plans Changed! 📱

A friend invites you out, but you have other plans. Choose a friendly answer.

Teacher: ask the student to say the selected response aloud and suggest an alternative.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Conversation Switch-Up 🔄

Choose a scenario. Talk for two minutes, then switch roles.

Old friends

Meet a friend after a few weeks. Talk about your weekend and your plans.

New classmates

Meet a new classmate. Find three things you both like.

Weekend planning

One person wants to go to the cinema. The other wants to stay home. Make a plan together.

Speaking challenge: Ask at least two follow-up questions. Use 'because' and 'What about you?'

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Conversation Starter Pack ✍️

Write three friendly questions for a new friend. Try to use what, where, when, or how.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

The Natural Conversation Challenge 🏆

Keep a conversation going for two minutes!

1. Start with a genuine question.

2. Share a personal experience.

3. React with 'Really?', 'Cool!', or 'That's nice!'

4. Ask two follow-up questions.

5. Ask 'What about you?' to keep talking.
