MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

The Opinion Spark ⚡

React naturally: give your opinion, explain why, and ask your partner a question. There are no right or wrong opinions.

Keep talking: “Personally, I think…” → “For example…” → “What about you?” → “Why do you think that?”
MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

The Hot Take Dictionary 🔥

Reveal each phrase, read the Portuguese meaning and example, and use it in your own opinion. Skip any card or the entire section.

Speaking challenge: Describe something overrated and something underrated. Explain your choices without insulting people who disagree.
MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Make Your Opinion Sound Natural 🧠

Comparatives

Free time is more valuable than expensive things.
Messaging is easier than calling.

Modals

People should take breaks.
Social media might be helpful.
We shouldn't judge everyone.

Contrast & concession

Although I understand your point, I disagree.
I like it. However, it can be expensive.

Respectful disagreement

That's a fair point, but...
I agree to some extent.
I see it differently because...

Notice: Support opinions with reasons and examples. Use might when you're not certain. Avoid extreme words like always and never unless you can justify them.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

The Friendly Disagreement 💬

Jordan has a strong opinion about social media. Choose your responses and see how the conversation changes. Retry to find more natural replies.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

The Perspective Switch 🎭

Teacher and student take turns playing these roles. Each round lasts 2–3 minutes. Switch positions halfway through.

Round 1: “Online friends can be as close as in-person friends.”

Round 2: “A good friend should always tell the truth.”

Round 3: “Doing nothing on the weekend is a good use of time.”

Rules: Ask at least two follow-up questions, give one example, acknowledge one fair point, and avoid personal attacks.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Hot Take Manifesto 📝

Create a short opinion post that could start a respectful conversation. Use a clear claim, a reason, an example and a concession.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

The 3-Minute Opinion Challenge 🏆

A surprising opinion. A real conversation.

Final prompt: “People should stop trying to be productive all the time.”

Take 30 seconds to prepare. Then speak for three minutes with your teacher. Explain your opinion, give an example, respond to a disagreement, ask two follow-up questions, and find one point of agreement.

B1 stretch: Use a comparative, a modal, although or however, and to some extent.
