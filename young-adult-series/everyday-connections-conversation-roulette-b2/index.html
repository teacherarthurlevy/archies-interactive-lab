MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Catch Up! ☕

Imagine you are catching up after six months. Move beyond basic updates: explore changes, unexpected experiences, and what you have learned. Ask follow-up questions.

What has changed most in your life over the past six months?
What is something you have recently reconsidered or changed your mind about?
What experience has had an unexpected impact on you?

Teacher: aim for 4–6 minutes of authentic exchange. Push for examples, comparisons, nuance, and thoughtful follow-ups.

MISSION 02 · SIX USEFUL EXPRESSIONS

Six High-Impact Expressions 🃏

Tap each compact card to reveal its meaning, Portuguese translation, and example. Then use each expression to share a genuine experience.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Make Conversation Flow Naturally 💬

React naturally: “That's fascinating!” · “I can imagine!” · “That must have been quite an experience.”

Ask for details: “How did that come about?” · “What did you take away from it?”

Share your experience: “That resonates with me because...” · “I've been through something similar, although...”

Keep it flowing: “That brings me to...” · “On a related note...”

Plans Changed! 📱

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Conversation Switch-Up 🔄

Choose a scenario. Talk for two minutes, then switch roles.

Old friends

Meet after years apart. Explore how your priorities, routines, and perspectives have evolved.

New classmates

Discover three unexpected connections while avoiding predictable interview-style questions.

Weekend planning

One person wants an adventurous trip; the other prefers a restorative weekend. Negotiate a plan that respects both needs.

Speaking challenge: Ask at least four probing follow-up questions, build on your partner's responses, and use two natural transition phrases.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Conversation Starter Pack ✍️

Write three thoughtful questions that invite stories, reflections, and detailed responses. Avoid predictable small talk.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

The Natural Conversation Challenge 🏆

Keep a conversation going for four minutes!

1. Start with a genuine question.

2. Share a personal experience.

3. React thoughtfully and build on your partner's answer.

4. Ask three meaningful follow-up questions.

5. Transition naturally with “That brings me to...” or “On a related note...”
