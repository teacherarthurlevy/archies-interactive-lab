MISSION 01 / 08

Let's Catch Up ☕

Start with a relaxed conversation. You don't need perfect English — just keep talking.

Life lately

How have you been? What's new in your life? What's something good that happened this week?

Work lately

How's work or studying going? Have you been busy? What's taking up most of your time?

Your energy

Are you more productive in the morning or at night? How do you recharge?

Small talk

What are you looking forward to? Is there anything you're trying to improve?

Conversation rule: Give an answer, add a detail, and ask a question back. Try: “Actually…”, “To be honest…”, “What about you?”

MISSION 03 / 08

What Are You Working On?

Present simple · routines

I usually start at nine.
I work with different people.
I don't work on weekends.

Present continuous · current projects

I'm working on a new project.
We're preparing for a meeting.
I'm not studying anything new right now.

Past simple · recent events

I had a busy day yesterday.
We solved a problem last week.

Future plans

I'm meeting a friend tonight.
I'm going to finish a task.
If I have time, I'll relax.

Notice: “I work in marketing” describes a regular situation. “I'm working on a campaign” describes a current project.

      MISSION 06 / 08

      Real Conversations, Real Situations

      ☕ The coffee break

      Meet a coworker you haven't spoken to in a while. Talk about current projects and weekend plans.

      📅 The quick check-in

      Tell your manager what you're working on, what you're responsible for, and what's next.

      🧩 The unexpected task

      A colleague needs help, but your schedule is full. Respond politely and suggest a realistic alternative.

      🌆 The after-work plan

      Invite a friend out, discuss what you like doing, and negotiate a day and time.

      Fluency challenge: Speak for 90 seconds about your week without reading notes. Use “usually”, “currently”, “last week”, and “next weekend”.
      MISSION 07 / 08

      My Week in Words ✍️

      Write a short personal update you could share with a friend or coworker. Then say it naturally without reading.

      MISSION 08 / 08

      Final Quest: Let's Catch Up! 🎙️

      Imagine you're catching up with an international friend you haven't seen in months. Speak for two to three minutes.

      1. Say what your life is like these days.
      2. Explain what you're currently working on.
      3. Describe one recent challenge.
      4. Share a plan for this week.
      5. Talk about something fun outside work.
      6. Ask your friend two natural questions.
