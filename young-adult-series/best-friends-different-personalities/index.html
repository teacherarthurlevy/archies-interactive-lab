Mission 01 · Warm-up & Engagement

Who Knows Who Better? 👀

A conversation for two best friends and their teacher. Take turns answering, guessing, and explaining why.

💬 Friendship check

How did you meet? What was your first impression? What makes your friendship special?

🎤 Teacher joins in

Teacher: Guess which student is more likely to be late, plan a trip, or start a conversation. Students: agree or disagree and give examples.

Sentence starters: “I think Mariana is more… because…” · “Actually, I'm usually…” · “We're both…” · “I don't agree because…”
Mission 02 · Vocabulary Discovery

Eight Personalities, Eight Visual Scenes 🎨

Look at the illustrated situation, guess the personality trait, and reveal the meaning. Compare yourselves after each card.

VISUAL STORY CARD

Teacher: “Who is more like this?” “Can you give me a real example?” “Is this trait always positive?”
Mission 03 · Language Focus

We're Similar, But Not the Same 🧩

Practice natural comparisons and describing routines. Answer the questions, then make your own examples aloud.

Notice the patterns

Comparative: “Mariana is more outgoing than me.” / “Vitória is quieter than Mariana.”
Both: “We're both supportive.”
Different: “I'm more organized, but she's more spontaneous.”
As … as: “I'm not as patient as she is.”
Frequency: “I usually plan ahead, but she sometimes decides at the last minute.”

Language note: short adjectives often take -er (quieter, funnier); longer ones often take more (more outgoing, more organized). We usually say more patient.

Speaking upgrade: After choosing an answer, say one true sentence about your friendship.
Mission 04 · Main Interactive Challenge

Who's More Likely To…? 🎯

Take turns choosing Vitória, Mariana, both, or the teacher. You must explain your choice and tell a real story if possible.

There are no right or wrong personalities. Useful phrases: “I would say…” · “Definitely!” · “It depends…” · “We're equally likely to…”
Mission 05 · Dialogue Lab

Best Friends: A Real Conversation 💬

Exactly ten dialogue lines, alternating between two best friends. No multiple-choice boxes. Reveal the conversation line by line, then perform it together.

10-LINE DIALOGUE

🎭 Read, swap, personalize

Round 1: Vitória reads Friend A and Mariana reads Friend B.
Round 2: Swap roles and read again.
Round 3: Replace two lines with real information about yourselves.

Teacher joins: “Who do you agree with?” “What would you do differently?” “What happened the last time you made plans together?”

Mission 06 · Communicative Practice

The Friendship Talk Show 🎙️

Three people, three roles. The teacher is the host; Vitória and Mariana are the guests. Switch roles whenever you want.

Host's follow-up: “Can you give us an example?” “Has that ever caused a problem?”
Guests' challenge: Ask each other at least one spontaneous question.
Mission 07 · Create & Apply

Our Friendship Survival Guide ✍️

Work together to write a mini guide for two people with different personalities. Make it funny, honest, and useful.

Present your guide in 60–90 seconds. The teacher can challenge one idea: “What if your friend doesn't agree?”
Mission 08 · Final Quest & Reflection

Different Personalities, Stronger Friendship? 🏆

Finish with a genuine conversation rather than memorized answers.

🎤 Final Quest — The Friendship Debate

Statement: “Best friends don't need to have similar personalities.”

Vitória: Give your opinion with one comparison.
Mariana: Agree or disagree and give a personal example.
Teacher: Challenge both opinions and ask a follow-up.

Then switch sides: Can you defend the opposite opinion?
