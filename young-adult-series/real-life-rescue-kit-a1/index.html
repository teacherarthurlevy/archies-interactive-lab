MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Small Problems, Big English ⚡

Talk about real situations. There is no perfect answer: communicate your idea!

Useful starts: I usually... / I need... / I ask... / I think...

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

The Everyday Rescue Kit 🧰

Ten useful words and expressions for real problems. Reveal the Portuguese meaning, speak the example, then make your own sentence.

Challenge: Choose three words and describe when you need them.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Help! What Can I Say? 🗣️

Ask for help

Can you help me?
Can I borrow your charger?
Can you repeat that, please?

Explain a problem

I can't find my wallet.
My phone is dead.
This is the wrong order.

Ask for information

Where is the outlet?
How much is it?
Does this bus go downtown?

Be polite

Excuse me...
Please.
Thank you!
Sorry, I don't understand.

Notice: After can, use the base verb: “Can you help me?” not “Can you helps me?” Use I'm for your situation and this is for an object or order.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

You're at a café with a dead phone. Choose your words and read the employee's reaction. Each answer has a different consequence.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

The Help Desk Challenge 🎭

One student has a problem. The other is the helper. Swap roles every two minutes. In a one-to-one class, the teacher plays the helper.

Card A · At a café

You need an outlet and a charger. Ask politely.

Card B · At a store

You have the wrong size. Ask for another size.

Card C · In a new city

You're lost. Ask for the bus stop.

Card D · At a restaurant

You received the wrong meal. Explain the problem.

Rules: Explain the problem, ask for help, ask one follow-up question, and thank the helper. The helper should ask one clarification question.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

Survive Three Surprises! 🏆

90-second rescue mission

The teacher chooses three surprise situations. For each one, explain the problem and ask for help in English.

You must use: 3 vocabulary items, 2 polite requests with Can..., 1 question with Where... or How much..., and 1 thank-you.

Bonus: React when the helper says “Sorry, I don't understand.”
