01 · Warm-up & Engagement

Would You Rather Travel…?

Choose one option and explain why. Use “I prefer…” or “I think… because…”

🏖️ Beach or city?

Which vacation is more relaxing? Which is more exciting?

🚆 Train or plane?

Which is faster? Which is more comfortable?

🏨 Hotel or apartment?

Which is better for a long trip?

🛍️ Shopping mall or local market?

Where can you find the best souvenirs?

Speaking mission: Choose a dream destination. Give three reasons you want to visit it. Ask your teacher two questions about their travel preferences.
02 · Vocabulary Discovery

Pack Your Travel Vocabulary

Read each flashcard, guess the meaning, then reveal the definition, Portuguese translation and example. Skip any card or the whole section.

Try it aloud: “The local market is more crowded than the museum.” Create three comparisons with today's vocabulary.
03 · Language Focus

Comparatives & Superlatives

Compare TWO things

Short: cheap → cheaper than
big → bigger than
easy → easier than

Long: more expensive than
more interesting than

Choose ONE from a group

Short: the cheapest
the biggest
the easiest

Long: the most expensive
the most interesting

Irregular forms

good → better → the best
bad → worse → the worst
far → farther/further → the farthest/furthest

Travel sentence starters

“I think ___ is better than ___ because…”
“___ is the cheapest option.”
“I'd rather visit ___ because…”

Notice: Use than after a comparative, and usually the before a superlative. “More cheaper” is incorrect; say “cheaper.”

04 · Main Interactive Challenge

Destination Decision Game 🌎

Compare these fictional classroom travel offers. The prices and descriptions are examples, not live travel quotes.

Option A

🏖️ Beach Getaway

3 nights · $240
Warm weather · Beach walks · Relaxing activities

Option B

🏙️ Big City Break

3 nights · $310
Museums · Restaurants · Shopping · Busy streets

Option C

⛰️ Mountain Escape

3 nights · $270
Hiking · Nature · Cool weather · Quiet evenings

Option D

🏛️ Historic Town

3 nights · $260
Old buildings · Local markets · Walking tours

Round 1 · Choose your trip

Choose one destination to begin.

Round 2 · Defend your choice

Make three comparisons and one superlative. Example: “The beach trip is cheaper than the city break. I think it's the best choice for me.”

Round 3 · Change the rules

Your travel partner prefers museums and restaurants. Would you keep your choice or change it? Explain in 45 seconds.

05 · Branching Conversation

Where Are We Going? 🗺️

Explore a fictional city. Your decisions change your travel plan. After every choice, explain your reason aloud.

06 · Communicative Practice

The City Mini-Debates 🎤

Teacher vs. student: each person chooses a different option. Use comparisons, ask follow-up questions and reach an agreement.

☕ Café vs. restaurant

Which is cheaper, faster, or better for lunch?

🚌 Bus vs. taxi

Which is more convenient for tourists?

🎟️ Museum vs. theme park

Which is more fun or more educational?

🧳 Carry-on vs. large suitcase

Which is more practical for a short trip?

Debate toolkit: “I see your point, but…” · “That's true. However…” · “What about…?” · “I agree because…” · “Let's choose…”

Challenge: Give at least two reasons, ask one question, and use one superlative in every debate.

07 · Create & Apply

Plan Your Perfect Day Abroad

Create a one-day city itinerary with a fictional $100 budget. Choose transport, two attractions, lunch and one souvenir.

Present it: Speak for 60–90 seconds. Your teacher is your travel partner and may ask you to change one activity.
08 · Final Quest & Reflection

Final Quest: Your Travel Advisor Challenge 🏆

You're the travel expert!

Your client wants an exciting day in a new city but has limited money. Recommend a destination, transport, two places to visit, a restaurant and a souvenir.

Success checklist: Use at least 4 comparatives, 2 superlatives, 5 travel words, and 2 reasons with “because.” Answer 3 follow-up questions.
