MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Ask Better Questions ❓

Do + I/you/we/they

Do you work?
Do they study?
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Does + he/she/it

Does she like music?
Does he work here?
Yes, she does. / No, he doesn't.

Wh- questions

Where do you live?
What do you do?
When do you study?
Who do you live with?

Keep it going

Really?
Why?
What about you?
And you?

Notice: After does, use the base verb: Does she like music? (not “Does she likes”). With be, ask “Are you a student?” — not “Do you are...”.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

Meet Taylor at College 🎭

Choose a reply. Each choice gets a different reaction. Read both roles aloud and try again.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Find Three Things in Common 🔎

Role-play A: You meet a new student. Role-play B: You meet a new coworker. Change roles after five minutes.

Ask at least six questions:

1. What's your name?
2. Where do you live?
3. Do you work or study?
4. What do you do in your free time?
5. Do you like movies or music?
6. What's your favorite food?
7. When do you usually relax?
8. What about you?

Rules: Ask two follow-up questions; react with “Really?” or “That's interesting!”; find three things you both like or do. For one-to-one classes, the teacher plays two different characters.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Conversation Passport 🪪

Create a short introduction and prepare five questions for someone new. Then use your passport in a real conversation.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

The 3-Minute First Meeting 🏆

Can you keep a conversation going?

Introduce yourself, ask five different questions, use Do/Does correctly, ask two follow-up questions, and find two things in common. Don't read a script!

Bonus: Ask your teacher to pretend to be a new classmate with surprising interests.
