MISSION 04 / 08

Language for Plans, Habits & Possibilities

Want to + verb

“I want to read more.”

Use this to express a goal or desire.

Going to + verb

“I'm going to practice every morning.”

Use this for a planned action.

Could + verb

“You could listen to a podcast while walking.”

Use this for a suggestion or possibility.

Look forward to + -ing / noun

“I'm looking forward to spending more time outside.”

Use this to express positive anticipation.

If + present, will + verb

“If I miss a day, I'll try again tomorrow.”

Use this for realistic future possibilities.

Frequency & routines

“I usually read in the evening.”

Use the present simple for habits.

Notice: “I want to improve” is a desire. “I'm going to practice for five minutes after breakfast” is a more concrete plan.

The Realistic Goals Challenge 🎯

Scenario: You're a busy young adult balancing work, study, friends, and rest. You want to improve your English this month without creating a stressful schedule.

Choose up to three habits

Your real week

Monday–Friday: Work or study takes most of the day.

Two evenings: Family or social commitments.

Weekend: Some flexibility, but you need rest.

Goal: Sustainable progress, not a perfect calendar.

Mini-debate: “Consistency matters more than intensity.” Agree or disagree? Give two reasons and an example.

MISSION 08 / 08

Final Quest: A Fresh Start Conversation 🌱

Imagine you're catching up with Sophia or Jake at the end of the month. Speak for three minutes about your goals, routines and experiences.

  1. Describe three things you wanted to improve.
  2. Explain which small habits you chose and why.
  3. Describe a challenge and how you handled it.
  4. Share something enjoyable that made your month meaningful.
  5. Say what you're looking forward to next month.
  6. Ask two natural follow-up questions.
