My Childhood Story · Adjectives · Action Verbs · Simple Past

ANYBODY CAN SPEAK ENGLISH, YOU JUST DON'T KNOW YET.
A1 · 8 missions · 50–60 minutes
MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

When I Was Little... 🧸

Talk about your childhood. Short answers are okay — add one detail.

Sentence starters: When I was little, I was... / I liked... / I played...

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY

What Were You Like? 🎈

Reveal each adjective, say the example, then make your own childhood sentence.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Simple Past, Made Simple ⏳

Was / Were

I was shy.
My friends were funny.
I wasn't quiet.
Were you playful?

Regular action verbs

play → played
watch → watched
walk → walked
visit → visited

Irregular action verbs

go → went
have → had
eat → ate
meet → met

Negatives & questions

I didn't play soccer.
Did you watch cartoons?
Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

Choose the correct answer

MISSION 04 · MEMORY CHALLENGE

Three Childhood Memories 📸

Read the memory, choose the best Past Simple sentence, and explain why.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

Old Friends, New Stories 💬

Choose a natural response and notice your friend's reaction.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

The Childhood Interview 🎤

Round A: Teacher interviews student. Round B: Swap roles.

Ask and answer:

1. Where did you live as a child?
2. What were you like?
3. What did you do after school?
4. Did you have a favorite game?
5. Who did you play with?
6. What food did you like?

Follow-up practice: Really? Why? Who with? How old were you?

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Childhood Time Capsule ✍️

Write six to eight short sentences about your past life. Then present them aloud.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

Then & Now: My Story 🌟

Speak for 60–90 seconds about your childhood.

Include two adjectives, three past action verbs, one negative sentence, and one comparison with your life now.

Starter: When I was little, I was... I played... I didn't... Now, I...
