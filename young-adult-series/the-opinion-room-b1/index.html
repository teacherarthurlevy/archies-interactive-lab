ANYBODY CAN SPEAK ENGLISH, YOU JUST DON'T KNOW YET.

Opinion Lab · 8 missions
Speaking confidence · giving reasons · polite disagreement
MISSION 01 · SPEAK

The First Impression

There are no perfect answers. Your job is to explain why.

Conversation starter

Follow-up: Can you think of a real example? Has your opinion changed over time?

MISSION 02 · NOTICE

Words That Make You Sound Confident

Reveal the meaning, use the expression in a sentence, and answer the personal question.

MISSION 03 · PRACTICE

Say It With a Reason

Formula: Opinion + Reason + Example

I'd rather live in a small city because it's calmer. For example, I can walk almost everywhere.

Speaking upgrade

Pick a quiz sentence and add a personal example. Try speaking for 30 seconds.

MISSION 04 · DECIDE

The Choice Machine

Choose your position, explain two reasons, then answer the twist.

MISSION 05 · RESPOND

The Group Chat

Your friend has a strong opinion. How will you respond? Each choice changes the conversation.

After each response, explain why you chose it. Try saying a better version out loud.

MISSION 06 · DEBATE

Change My Mind

Teacher: take the opposite position, even if you agree! Student: defend your view, acknowledge a counterargument, and respond.

Hot take

Conversation toolkit

“I see your point, but…” · “The main reason is…” · “For example…” · “On the other hand…” · “I might change my mind if…”

MISSION 07 · CREATE

My Personal Rulebook

Create three life rules you genuinely believe in. Each needs a reason and an example. Present them to your teacher.

Challenge: Which rule would your best friend disagree with? Why?

MISSION 08 · REVIEW

The Final Hot Take

“A comfortable life is better than an exciting life.”

Take a position. Give two reasons, a real-life example, and one counterargument. Speak for 90–120 seconds.

Confidence check

Student Activity Report
