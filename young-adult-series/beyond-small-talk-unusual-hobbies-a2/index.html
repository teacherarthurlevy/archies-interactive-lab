MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Beyond “What Do You Do?” 🧊

Talk about real interests, experiences and curiosity. Give an answer, a reason and one example.

Conversation ladder: Answer → add a reason → ask “What about you?” → ask a follow-up question.
MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

The Unusual Hobby Deck 🎨

Reveal a word, read the example, and describe who might enjoy the activity. Skip any card without losing progress.

Quick speaking challenge: Choose two hobbies you would try and one you probably wouldn't. Explain why respectfully.
MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Ask Better Questions ❓

Present Simple & frequency

How often do you go hiking?
I usually go twice a month.

Present Perfect experiences

Have you ever tried pottery?
No, I haven't, but I'd like to.

Preferences & reasons

I prefer painting to hiking.
I'm interested in photography because...

Invitations & follow-ups

Would you like to join us?
How did you get into that?
What do you like most about it?

Notice: Go + -ing for activities (go hiking); interested in + noun/-ing; Have you ever + past participle; would like to + base verb.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

Meet Alex at the Creative Market 💬

Choose a reply. See how Alex reacts. Notice which answers open up the conversation.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Find Your People 🤝

Imagine you're at a weekend hobby festival. You want to meet people, but everyone has different interests.

🥾 Hiking club

Ask about trails, equipment, experience and frequency.

🎨 Painting studio

Ask about materials, favorite subjects and beginner classes.

🔭 Stargazing group

Ask about locations, timing and what people can see.

🗺️ Geocaching group

Ask how it works, what you need and why people enjoy it.

Speaking task: Choose two clubs. For each one, introduce yourself, ask three open questions, react naturally, and suggest a future activity. Your teacher plays the club member and asks follow-up questions.
MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Connection Passport 🪪

Build a short profile to introduce yourself to people at a real event. You can invent details if you prefer.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

The 3-Minute Connection Challenge 🏆

Make a new friend — without small talk!

Your teacher plays a stranger who has an unexpected hobby. Introduce yourself, discover their interest, ask at least four follow-up questions, explain your own hobbies, and suggest something you could do together.

A2 stretch: Use Have you ever...?, How often...?, because, prefer, and Would you like to...?.
