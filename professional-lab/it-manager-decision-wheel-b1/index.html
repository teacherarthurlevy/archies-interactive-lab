MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Giving feedback & making requests

Describe the situation

“I've noticed that the last two reports were submitted late.”

Focus on facts, not personal criticism.
Explain the impact

“This delays the team's planning process.”

Explain why it matters.
Invite their perspective

“Is anything making this task difficult?”

Ask before assuming.
Agree on action

“Could we agree on a realistic deadline?”

Clarify ownership and timing.
Useful softeners: “Could we…?” · “Would it be possible to…?” · “I'd like us to…” · “What do you think about…?”
