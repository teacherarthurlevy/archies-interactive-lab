MISSION 01 / 08

How's Your Day Looking? ☕

Start with a real conversation. Use full answers and ask your teacher questions back.

Morning

How did your day start? What is your usual morning routine? What puts you in a good mood?

Work

What is on your plate today? Which customer or project needs attention? How many meetings do you have?

Life

What are your plans for tonight? How do you unwind? Are you trying to change any habits?

Work-life balance

When is your busiest time? What do you do when a meeting runs late? How do you protect personal time?

Speaking: Give a 60-second check-in: “Today is looking… My top priority is… After work, I'm hoping to…”

MISSION 03 / 08

Plans, Priorities & Expectations

Intentions

I'm planning to review customer accounts.
I'm going to prepare the renewal presentation.

Arrangements

I'm meeting the client at two.
I'm supposed to send the report today.

Priorities

My top priority is the renewal.
I'd rather handle this before lunch.

Flexibility

If something urgent comes up, I'll adjust.
I was going to work out, but my call ran late.

Notice: “I'm meeting” describes an arrangement; “I'm planning to meet” emphasizes intention; “I'm supposed to meet” expresses an expectation.

MISSION 06 / 08

Let's Just Talk 💬

Role-play these realistic conversations. Swap roles and ask natural follow-up questions.

Coffee chat

“Morning! How's your week going? Anything fun planned tonight?”

Connect professional and personal topics naturally.

Manager check-in

“What are your priorities today? Anything blocking you?”

Explain what needs attention and what can wait.

Customer follow-up

“Could you give us a quick update on our onboarding timeline?”

Set expectations without overpromising.

After-work invitation

“Are you free tonight? Want to grab something to eat?”

Accept, decline, or suggest another time.

Fluency round: Speak for two minutes about your actual day. Include a priority, an unexpected change, and an evening plan.
