MISSION 01 · THE OPINION SPARK · 8 MIN

What Would a Great CSM Say? ⚡

Start with your own experience. Give an opinion, a reason, and an example. The teacher should challenge your answer with a respectful follow-up.

Sentence starters: “In my opinion...” · “I partly agree because...” · “From the customer's perspective...” · “One example is...”
MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY · 10 MIN

The Customer Success Dictionary 🧠

Explain the term before revealing the Portuguese translation and workplace example. Then use the term in a sentence.

Speaking challenge: Explain the relationship between onboarding, adoption, and renewal.
MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS · 10 MIN

Disagree Without Losing Trust 💬

Compare:

❌ “You are wrong. We can't do that.”
✅ “I understand your concern. However, that feature is not available yet. Let's explore another option.”

❌ “It will be fixed tomorrow.”
✅ “I will share an update tomorrow, even if the fix is still in progress.”

Useful phrases: “I see your point, but...” · “What outcome are you trying to achieve?” · “I suggest...” · “If we can..., then...”

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION · 10 MIN

The Unhappy Customer Call ☎️

The client is frustrated. Choose a response and see the immediate reaction. Practice speaking your choice before clicking. Retry to explore different outcomes.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE · 9 MIN

Perspective Switch: CSM vs. Client 🎭

Play two rounds. First, the teacher is an unhappy customer and the student is the CSM. Then switch roles. Each person should defend their interests without becoming rude.

Role A — CSM

Protect trust, avoid false promises, understand the customer's business goals, and agree on a follow-up.

Role B — Customer

Your team is losing time. You need a workaround, a clear update, and a reason to renew.

Role C — Sales Director

You want to hit the expansion target this quarter. Challenge the CSM's recommendation.

Role D — Product Manager

You cannot promise the requested feature this quarter. Suggest alternatives.

Teacher challenge cards: “I want a discount today.” · “Your competitor can do this.” · “Why should we renew?” · “I want to speak to your manager.”
MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY · 6 MIN

The Account Recovery Playbook 📝

Write a short professional recovery plan for a fictional at-risk customer. Use the plan as the basis for a 60-second pitch.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST · 4 MIN

Save the Account — Final Pitch 🏆

Your 3-minute challenge

A strategic customer has low product adoption, a serious unresolved complaint, and a renewal meeting next month. The customer says: “Your competitor is cheaper. Why should we stay?”

Deliver a response with: empathy → diagnosis → value → realistic plan → follow-up. The teacher asks one unexpected follow-up.
