MISSION 02 · CYBERSECURITY CONTEXT

What Happened — and What Do We Know? 🔎

Reading · Publicly reported C-Track incident (2026)

Thomson Reuters disclosed unauthorized access involving files associated with its C-Track court case-management platform. The company investigated potential exposure of personal information and reported that the platform's operations had not been disrupted.

Important distinction: This reported incident is not proof that servers were frozen by ransomware. Treat any separate frozen-server account as unverified. Do not disclose confidential information.

For source verification: consult Thomson Reuters' official communications and contemporaneous reporting. This lesson is not a live incident-status feed.

Discuss:
  1. How does unauthorized access differ from ransomware?
  2. Why is a data breach serious even without an outage?
  3. How should engineering and security teams communicate uncertainty?
  4. What should customers be told, and when?
  5. What security lessons apply to everyday engineering?

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

The Leadership Update & Engineering Debate 🎙️

Teacher: CTO. Student: senior engineer. Give a 90-second incident update with facts, impact, current actions, uncertainty, and next checkpoint. Switch roles.

CTO asks: What's the biggest risk? Can we restore safely? How confident are you in that estimate? What do you need from leadership?

Debate: Should companies ever pay a ransomware demand? Is human error the biggest risk? Should security take priority over features? Has AI-assisted coding increased risk?

Challenge: Give a position, one example, one counterargument, and a conclusion.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

The 3-Minute Engineering Briefing 🏆

Communicate like a senior engineer.

Choose a non-confidential real project or the fictional incident. Present: (1) what has been happening, (2) the technical challenge, (3) your approach, (4) outcome/current status, and (5) next steps.

Respond to two difficult leadership questions without inventing facts or promising an unverified ETA.

Stretch: Use present perfect continuous, a conditional, and a hedging expression.
