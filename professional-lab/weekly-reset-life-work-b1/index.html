MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

The Weekly Reset Meeting 🎭

Round A · At work

Teacher: Manager who needs a progress update.

Student: IT manager balancing deadlines, team needs, and priorities.

Negotiate one deadline, explain a risk, and ask for support.

Round B · At home

Teacher: Family member planning the week.

Student: Someone who wants to protect personal time.

Discuss plans, suggest alternatives, and agree on one shared activity.

Speaking tools

My main concern is... / Could we move this to...? / I can handle... but I'll need help with... / I'd like to make time for... / How about...?

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

Build Your Week 🗓️

Create a realistic plan that includes work, personal life, and recovery time.

Speaking extension: Present your plan in one minute. Your teacher should challenge one of your decisions.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

The Week Ahead: Your 90-Second Briefing 🎯

Give a realistic update about your week.

Explain how you feel after the holiday, two work responsibilities, one personal goal, one possible problem, and what you'll do about it.

Use need to, going to, should, and at least three vocabulary expressions.
