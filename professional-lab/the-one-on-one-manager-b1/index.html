MISSION 01 · WARM-UP · 8 MIN

Inside the Manager's Office ☕

You are an IT manager. Talk about your experience leading developers and outsourced teams. Use real examples without sharing confidential information.

Teacher follow-ups: Can you give an example? How did the employee react? What would you change?

Speaking starter: “In my team, we usually...” / “One challenge I often face is...”

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY · 10 MIN

The Manager's Toolkit 🧰

Try to explain each expression in English before revealing its Portuguese meaning and example. Then create your own workplace sentence.

Mini speaking task: Explain the difference between feedback, recognition, and action items. Give an example from an IT team.
MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS · 10 MIN

Sound Supportive, Not Demanding 💬

Notice the difference:

❌ “You always miss deadlines.”
✅ “I noticed two tasks were late. What happened?”

❌ “Fix your communication.”
✅ “Could you send a short update every Tuesday?”

❌ “Work harder.”
✅ “What support would help you finish this task?”

Useful patterns: “I noticed that...” · “How do you feel about...?” · “Would you mind...?” · “Let's agree on...”

Choose the best expression

Receive immediate feedback. You can retry incorrect answers.

Speak: Turn “You're too slow” into a respectful, specific feedback sentence.
MISSION 04 · MAIN CHALLENGE · 15 MIN

The One-on-One Decision Room 🎯

Read the employee's situation. Choose an approach, explain why, then respond to the unexpected new information. Both approaches can be reasonable: defend your choice.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION · 10 MIN

A Difficult One-on-One 🤝

Your employee is overloaded. Choose your response and see how the conversation develops. Try different options and notice their effect on trust.

Reflect: Which response showed empathy? Which response created a clear next step? Try the dialogue again using your own words.
MISSION 06 · ROLE-PLAY · 10 MIN

Manager vs. Employee: Switch Roles 🎭

Round 1: Teacher plays an employee. Student plays the manager. Hold a 4-minute one-on-one. Round 2: Switch roles and respond to a new concern.

Meeting structure:
  1. Check in: “How have things been going?”
  2. Explore: “What's been challenging?”
  3. Feedback: “I noticed...”
  4. Support: “How can I help?”
  5. Agree: “Let's check in next week.”
Teacher challenge: Interrupt politely, disagree with the feedback, or ask for an unrealistic promise.
MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY · 7 MIN

Build Your One-on-One Meeting Plan 📝

Prepare a realistic meeting with a developer or outsourced collaborator. Use fictional names and non-confidential examples.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST · 5 MIN

Lead the Meeting with Confidence 🏆

Final 3-minute simulation

The teacher plays a developer who has missed deadlines and feels overwhelmed. The student must open the meeting, ask two open questions, offer one piece of respectful feedback, suggest support, and agree on two action items.

Extra challenge: The employee says, “I don't think this is fair.” Respond calmly and ask for their perspective.
