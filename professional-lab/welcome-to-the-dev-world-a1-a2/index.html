01 · Welcome & Engagement

Hi! Nice to Meet You 👋

Let's have a natural conversation. Short answers are fine, but try to add one more detail.

About you

What's your name? Where are you from? What do you do? How old are you? Where do you live?

Starter: “I'm… I'm from… I'm a software developer.”

Your free time

What do you like doing after work? Do you play games, watch movies, exercise, or travel?

Starter: “I like… I usually…”

Your work

What do you build? What programming languages do you use? Do you work alone or with a team?

Starter: “I work with… I build…”

Your future

Why do you want to learn English? What kind of international job do you want?

Starter: “I want to… because…”

Teacher observation: Listen for spontaneous responses, comprehension of simple questions, verb to be, present simple, vocabulary range and confidence. Do not interrupt every error.

Quick choice · Which sounds more like you?

Quiet or talkative? Creative or practical? Morning person or night owl? Teamwork or working alone? Explain one choice.

02 · Vocabulary Discovery

My Personality & My Work

Discover useful traits and action verbs. Reveal the meaning, Portuguese translation and developer-related example. Use each new word in a personal sentence.

Speaking: Choose 3 traits that describe you and 3 action verbs that describe your work. Example: “I'm curious and patient. I build websites and fix bugs.”
03 · Language Focus

Verb to Be + Developer Action Verbs

Who you are · be

I'm a developer.
I'm creative.
My team is friendly.
We are organized.

Negative: I'm not shy.
Question: Are you patient?

What you do · present simple

I write code.
I test applications.
I work remotely.
She builds websites.

Negative: I don't use Java.
Question: Do you use Python?

Talk about likes

I like technology.
I like coding.
I don't like long meetings.
Do you like teamwork?

Talk about goals

I want to improve my English.
I want to work abroad.
I want to join an international team.

Notice: “I am a developer” describes your identity. “I develop software” describes an action. Avoid “I am develop software.”

04 · Main Speaking Challenge

Meet the Developer 🎙️

Imagine you're meeting a colleague from another country. Practice a short introduction and then answer natural follow-up questions.

Model dialogue

Alex: Hi! I'm Alex. I'm a software developer. What's your name?

You: Hi, I'm ____. Nice to meet you!

Alex: What do you do at work?

You: I ____ and ____.

Alex: What are you like as a teammate?

You: I'm ____ and ____.

Alex: What do you do after work?

You: I like ____.

Round 1 · A1 support

Introduce yourself in 4 sentences. Use “I'm,” “I work,” “I like,” and “I want.”

Round 2 · A2 extension

Give a 45–60 second introduction. Add reasons with “because,” describe a normal workday and ask the teacher two questions.

Teacher: Note whether the student needs sentence starters, can answer without translation, and can sustain follow-up conversation.

06 · Communicative Practice

Let's Talk: Developer & Everyday Life

☕ Coffee chat

You're having a break with a new colleague. Talk about hobbies, favorite apps, weekends and your city.

🧩 Daily stand-up

Say what you usually do at work. Explain one task you are working on using simple present language.

🤝 Team introduction

Tell a new teammate about your strengths and ask about theirs.

🎯 Career conversation

Tell a recruiter why you want to work internationally and which English skills you want to improve.

Teacher challenge: Ask “Why?”, “Can you give me an example?”, “How often?”, and “What about you?” Encourage longer answers without demanding advanced grammar.

Optional A2 stretch

Explain a simple technical problem: “Sometimes my code has a bug. First, I check the error. Then I test the solution.”

07 · Create & Apply

My Developer Profile 🚀

Create a short introduction for an international team. Use real information; don't invent skills or experience.

Speaking outcome: Present your profile without reading it. Your teacher will ask three follow-up questions.
08 · Level Check & Reflection

Final Quest: Your First Interview 🎯

Teacher = recruiter. Student = candidate. Keep the interview friendly and realistic.

Five interview questions

  1. Can you tell me about yourself?
  2. What do you do as a developer?
  3. What are your strengths?
  4. What technologies do you use?
  5. Why do you want an international job?

A2 extension: Describe a typical workday, explain a simple project, and ask the interviewer two questions.
