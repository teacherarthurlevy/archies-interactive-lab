Mission 03 · Language Focus

Feedback That Moves Things Forward

Practice describing trends, asking questions, giving feedback, and agreeing on next steps.

Useful language patterns

Describe a trend: “Response time increased from 12 to 30 hours.”
Ask without blaming: “What do you think contributed to this?”
Acknowledge: “I can see how frustrating that must be.”
Suggest: “We could review the workflow together.”
Set an action: “Let's agree on two steps and review progress next Friday.”
Be realistic: “I can't promise an immediate fix, but I'll keep you updated.”

Make the feedback constructive

Rewrite: “You're too slow.” · “Stop making mistakes.” · “Customers keep complaining about you.”
