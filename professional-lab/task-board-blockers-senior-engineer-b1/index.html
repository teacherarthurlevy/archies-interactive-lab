MISSION 01 · WARM-UP · 8 MIN

What's on Your Board Today? 📋

Talk about sprint planning, task cards, and the responsibilities of a senior engineer. Use fictional examples instead of confidential project details.

Teacher follow-ups: Who owns the card? What's the impact? How would you explain this to a nontechnical stakeholder?

Speaking starter: “I'm currently working on...” / “The main blocker is...” / “We're waiting for...”

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY · 10 MIN

Task Board Language 🧩

Explain the term in English first. Reveal the Portuguese translation and example. Use it in a short stand-up update.

Speak: “Card ENG-155 is blocked because... We need... Our next step is...”
MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS · 10 MIN

Explain Blockers Clearly 💬

Notice:

❌ “The card no work.”
✅ “The card is blocked because we're waiting for API access.”

❌ “I finish tomorrow, sure.”
✅ “I expect to finish tomorrow if the dependency is resolved.”

❌ “You need do this.”
✅ “Could you clarify the acceptance criteria?”

Useful patterns: “We're waiting for...” · “This depends on...” · “I suggest...” · “If we get access, we can...”

10 quick language decisions

Speak: Give a 30-second update with status, blocker, owner, and next action.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION · 10 MIN

The Blocker Escalation Meeting 🔀

You're the senior engineer. The product owner wants answers about a blocked payment integration. Choose how to communicate the risk and next steps.

Speak: After choosing a response, say it again in your own words. Try a calm, confident tone without overpromising.
MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE · 10 MIN

Stand-Up, Triage & Cross-Team Sync 🎙️

Round 1: Give a 60-second stand-up: Yesterday / Today / Blockers. Round 2: Teacher plays the product owner and challenges your estimate. Round 3: Coordinate a frontend-backend dependency.

Teacher prompts: “Why is the card still blocked?” “Can we deliver on Friday?” “What is the smallest safe solution?” “Who owns the next action?” “What happens if the dependency is late?”
MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY · 7 MIN

Write a Professional Task Update ✍️

Create a clear task-card comment and use it to brief the product owner. Your update should be useful even to someone who wasn't in the meeting.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST · 5 MIN

The Senior Engineer's Sprint Briefing 🏆

3-minute final simulation

You have three cards: ENG-142 (bug fix in progress), ENG-155 (payment integration blocked), and ENG-173 (frontend dependency). Brief the product owner on priorities, blockers, owners, realistic estimates, and next steps.

Twist: “The client wants everything by Friday.” Negotiate a clear, responsible response.

Success: Communicate status + reason + impact + action + next update.
