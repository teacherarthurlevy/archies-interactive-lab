Mission 02 · Vocabulary

Words About Me

Learn simple personality words and daily actions.

Speak: “I am…” and “I…” Use three new words.
Mission 03 · Language Focus

I Am / I Do

Use am, is, and are to describe people. Use simple verbs for routines.

Personality: I am calm. · You are active. · She is organized. · He is friendly.

Routine: I cook dinner. · I do the dishes. · She cooks dinner. · He does the dishes.

Questions: Are you organized? · Do you cook dinner?

Mission 04 · Meet the People

Four People, Four Routines

Read short first-person introductions. Discover how their personalities and routines are different.

Speaking: Who is like you? “I am like Julia. I am organized.” Who is different?
Mission 05 · Who Is It?

Guess the Person

Read a clue. Choose the correct person.

After every answer, say: “It is ___ because ___.”
Mission 06 · Talk to a Person

Hello! Nice to Meet You

Choose what to say. See the other person's answer.

Role-play

Teacher: “Hello! My name is Clara. What is your name?”

Student: “Hi! My name is ___. Nice to meet you.”

Teacher: “Are you calm? What do you do after work?”

Student: “I am ___. I ___.”

Introduce Yourself!
