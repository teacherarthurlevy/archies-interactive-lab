Language focus

Teacher's guide · Suggested pacing

75–90 min: Warm-up 8, vocabulary 8, language 10, real work update 18, branching 10, personal life/debate 15, outlook 10, final 8. For shorter classes, use mission navigation and skip controls.

Do not invent projects, deadlines, teams, personal circumstances, or holiday plans for the learner. Follow the student's actual topics. Ask for reasons, examples, clarification, and follow-up questions. Allow learners to decline personal questions. No audio, recording, or text-to-speech.
