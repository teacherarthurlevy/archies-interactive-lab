MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

When Life Goes Off Script ⚡

There are no perfect answers. Explain your experience, react to the teacher, and ask one follow-up question.

The B1 Rescue Vocabulary Kit 🧰

Reveal the meaning and example. Make a new sentence about a realistic problem. Skip freely when necessary.

Mini-challenge: Use run out of, figure out and backup plan in one short story.
MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Say It Better 🧠

Polite requests

Could you explain what happened?
Would you mind checking my order?

First conditional

If the bus doesn't arrive, we'll take a taxi.

Advice

If I were you, I'd contact support.
You should ask for help.

Past regrets

I should have checked the address.
We shouldn't have waited so long.

Notice: Would you mind + -ing. Should have + past participle. If + present simple, will + base verb for real future possibilities.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Rescue Hotline: Live Role-Play 📞

Teacher = the person with the problem. Student = the problem solver. Swap roles after each round.

Round A · The Lost Backpack

Your friend left a backpack on a bus. Ask for details, suggest three actions, and give a backup plan.

Round B · The Failed Group Chat

Someone misunderstood a message and now two friends are upset. Clarify what happened and suggest a compromise.

Round C · The Event Disaster

It's raining and the outdoor school event starts in 30 minutes. Negotiate a plan B.

B1 challenge: Ask at least three clarification questions, give two realistic solutions, justify your preferred solution, and respond to one objection.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

90 Seconds to Save the Day 🏆

Final challenge: a surprise problem!

Your friend is lost, their phone has died, and you have 15 minutes before a school trip leaves. Explain the situation, give a realistic plan, make a polite request, and explain what you'll do if your first plan fails.

B1 stretch: Include a first conditional, one polite request, one phrasal verb, and one sentence expressing regret.
