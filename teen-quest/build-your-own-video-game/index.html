Mission 01 · Warm-up

Welcome, Game Designer!

Talk about games you love and what makes them exciting.

Speaking support: “My favorite game is…” · “I like it because…” · “In this game, players can…” · “I would change…”
Mission 02 · Vocabulary

The Game Designer's Dictionary

Learn the words you need to build and describe a video game.

Speaking: Choose three words and use them to describe a game you know.
Mission 03 · Language Focus

Talk Like a Game Creator

Practice describing abilities, rules, comparisons, and sequences.

Power-up phrases

Ability: “My hero can jump over walls.”
Rules: “Players have to collect three keys.”
Advice: “You should avoid the dragon.”
Comparison: “The robot is faster than the monster.”
Instructions: “First, find the map. Then, open the door. Finally, defeat the boss.”

Speaking challenge

Explain two rules for your game using have to and can't.

Mission 04 · Character Builder

Create Your Main Hero

Choose a character, assign skill points, and give your hero a strength and weakness.

Mission 05 · World Builder

Talk to Characters
