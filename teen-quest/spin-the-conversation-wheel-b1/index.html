MISSION 01 · WARM UP

What Makes a Great Conversation? ⚡

Choose a side. Explain your opinion and ask a follow-up question.

Would you rather...

Have no phone for a week OR no games for a month?

Agree or disagree?

Teenagers should decide their own screen-time limits.

Choose one!

Be famous online OR be successful in private?

Teacher: model “I agree because...” and ask “Why?” or “Can you give an example?”

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY

The Conversation Toolkit 🧰

Reveal the meaning, example, and Portuguese translation. Skip any card without earning XP.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Sound Confident, Not Rude 💬

Opinion: “In my opinion...” / “I believe that...”

Reason: “The main reason is...” / “For example...”

Polite disagreement: “I see your point, but...”

Follow-up: “What makes you think that?”

MISSION 04 · MAIN GAME

The Conversation Roulette 🎡

How to play: Spin, read the question, speak for 45–60 seconds, then answer a surprise follow-up. The teacher can spin again, skip, or choose a topic. Spinning alone does not award XP.

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

The Group Chat Dilemma 📱

Your friend wants to post a funny but embarrassing video of a classmate. Choose your response and see what happens.

Teacher: after each choice ask, “Why did you say that?” and “What could happen next?”

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATE

Perspective Switch 🎭

Pick a role, defend your position, then switch sides.

Scenario A · Phone rules

Student: “Phones help us learn.” Parent: “Phones distract us.” Negotiate a fair rule.

Scenario B · AI homework

Student: “AI is a helpful tool.” Teacher: “Students need to think independently.” Agree on guidelines.

Scenario C · Weekend plans

Friend A wants a party. Friend B wants a quiet evening. Find a compromise.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

Make Your Own Hot Take 🔥

Write an original statement that people could reasonably disagree about. Add your opinion and one question for the other person.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

The 2-Minute Conversation Champion 🏆

Your final mission:

1. Spin the roulette or choose your favorite topic.

2. State your opinion and give two reasons.

3. Respond politely to a different opinion.

4. Ask two follow-up questions.
