The Day Everything Went Wrong! 🔎

Story Detective · Past Simple · Unexpected adventures

ANYBODY CAN SPEAK ENGLISH, YOU JUST DON'T KNOW YET.
8 missions · 50–60 minutes
Understand → Notice → Practice → Create → Speak → Review
MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Have You Ever Had a Terrible Day? 😅

Tell a short story. It can be real or imaginary.

Sentence starters: Yesterday, I... / Last weekend, I... / First... Then... Finally...

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

Words That Make Stories Better 📚

Reveal each word, say the example, and create your own sentence.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Past Simple: What Happened?

Regular verbs

play → played · miss → missed · stop → stopped · study → studied

Irregular verbs

go → went · lose → lost · find → found · see → saw · have → had · get → got

Negative and questions

I didn't find my phone. · Did you see my bag? · What did you do?

Sequence your story

First, I left home. Then, I missed the bus. Suddenly, it rained. Luckily, my friend helped. Finally, I arrived.

Interactive grammar check

MISSION 04 · STORY DETECTIVE

The Mystery of the Missing Backpack 🕵️

Investigate five clues. Reconstruct what happened in the correct order.

Detective tip: Use the time and a Past Simple verb to explain this clue.
MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

Something Went Wrong! 💬

Choose what to say to your friend. Notice the reaction and try again.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Disaster Decisions 🎭

Choose the best next action and explain it in English. Then retell what happened using the Past Simple.

Role-play: The Witness

Teacher = reporter. Student = witness. Answer: Where were you? What happened first? What did you do next? How did it end? Swap roles.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

Write Your Own Disaster Story ✍️

Create a funny or surprising story (real or fictional). Aim for 70–100 words.

Speaking extension: Tell the story without reading. Your teacher will ask three follow-up questions.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST

The 90-Second Story Challenge 🏆

Tell the story of a day when everything went wrong!

Include four Past Simple verbs, three sequencing words, one unexpected problem, and one ending. Answer two questions from your teacher.
