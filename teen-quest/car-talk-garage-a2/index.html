CAR TALK 🚗

The Ultimate Garage Challenge · Parts, verbs, personal taste & real-life conversations

ANYBODY CAN SPEAK ENGLISH, YOU JUST DON'T KNOW YET.
8 missions · 50–60 minute speaking-first lesson

Understand → Notice → Practice → Create → Speak → Review

01 · WARM-UP & ENGAGEMENT

Welcome to the Garage! 🚗

Let's talk about cars, style, and your dream ride. Give reasons, not just short answers.

Sentence starters: I prefer… / My favorite car is… / I like it because… / I don't really like…

02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

Know Your Car Parts 🛠️

Seven interactive cards: identify the part, reveal its meaning, then speak.

03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Talk Like a Car Fan

Useful verbs

drive, park, turn, stop, start, check, repair, choose, buy, prefer.

I drive to school. · She drives a small car. · Check the tires. · I prefer blue cars.

Express your taste

I like sports cars. · I don't like very big cars. · I'd prefer a black car. · I'd rather have a comfortable car.

Compare cars

This car is cheaper than that one. · An SUV is usually bigger than a hatchback. · This car is more comfortable than that car.

Safety language: Wear your seat belt. Stop at a red light. Ask a qualified adult or mechanic about vehicle problems.

Check your English

Speaking: Compare two imaginary cars using cheaper, bigger, faster, more comfortable.

04 · MAIN INTERACTIVE CHALLENGE

The Car Choice Challenge 🏁

Make decisions about budget, style, safety, and comfort. Explain each choice aloud.

05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

At the Car Dealership 💬

You are the customer. Choose natural English and see the salesperson's reaction.

Teacher extension: Repeat the conversation without reading the choices.

06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Car Show: Convince Me! 🎤

Round 1 — The Interview

Teacher: You're a car show host. Ask: What kind of car do you like? Why? What's your favorite color? Which is better: electric or gasoline? What is your budget?

Student: Answer in full sentences. Use prefer, because, cheaper than, more comfortable than.

Round 2 — Swap roles

The student becomes the host and asks at least five questions. The teacher is a customer with unusual preferences.

Round 3 — The Road Trip

Plan a safe trip for three friends. Choose a car, decide what to pack in the trunk, and explain which parts you should check before leaving.

07 · CREATE & APPLY

Design Your Dream Car ✨

You're launching a new car. Make an advertisement for teenage car fans (future drivers!).

Pitch: Present your car in 60–90 seconds. The teacher asks two follow-up questions.

08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

The Ultimate Garage Challenge 🏆

Sell your dream car to a customer!

Speak for 90 seconds. Mention three parts, four verbs, two preferences, and one comparison. Then answer: Why should I choose your car?
