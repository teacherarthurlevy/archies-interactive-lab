MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Who Are You, Really? 👀

Talk about yourself. Give a short answer, then add an example. Your teacher can ask: Why? or Can you tell me more?

Level ladder: A1: “I'm creative.” · A1+: “I draw pictures.” · A2: “I'm creative because I draw and design things.”
MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

Personality + Action Verbs 🧩

Discover 10 personality traits and 10 action verbs. Reveal, say the example aloud, and make your own sentence. Skipping gives no XP.

Action verb bank:

Speaking challenge: Choose a trait and match it to an action. Example: “I'm helpful. I help my friends.”

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Who We Are + What We Do 🧠

BE + trait

I am curious.
She is friendly.
They are creative.
Are you shy?

Present Simple + action

I draw.
He draws.
She helps her friends.
Do you play games?

Skills

I can build things.
I'm good at drawing.
Can you solve puzzles?

A2 stretch

I'm patient because I listen.
I'm shy, but I like my friends.
I usually help my team.

Notice: Use be before an adjective (“She is kind”), but an action verb directly after the subject (“She helps”). Add -s for he/she/it in positive Present Simple.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Build the Dream Team 🚀

You are creating a team for a school project. You need three people with different skills and personalities.

🎨 The Creator

Creative. Draws and designs. Sometimes shy.

🧠 The Problem Solver

Curious and patient. Explores and solves problems.

🤝 The Team Helper

Friendly and helpful. Listens and shares ideas.

⚡ The Leader

Energetic and organized. Plans and presents.

Mission: Choose three team members. Explain what each person does and why your team works. Your teacher challenges your choices: “What if your team has no leader?”

Level ladder: A1: name traits and actions. A1+: use full sentences. A2: defend your team using because, but, can and follow-up questions.
MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Character Profile 🎭

Create a real or fictional version of yourself. You don't have to share personal details.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

Who Am I? The Final Challenge 🏆

A two-minute personality interview

Describe yourself with three traits and three action verbs. Describe a friend or fictional character. Ask your teacher two questions.

A1: 5–6 clear sentences.
A1+: Include examples and one reason.
A2: Explain a contrast using but, give two reasons using because, and ask follow-up questions.
