MISSION 01 · WARM-UP

Welcome to Your Story! 👋

This isn't a test. Let's discover who you are! Answer aloud. The teacher can ask follow-up questions.

Level-up: answer with because!

MISSION 02 · VOCABULARY DISCOVERY

Talk About Yourself ✨

Reveal the meaning and example. Make your own sentence. You may skip a card or the whole section.

Level-up: Use I'm into..., I'm good at... and I'd like to... in three sentences.

MISSION 03 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

Speak About Your World 🧠

We recycle familiar English and add new ways to express ideas.

About me · Verb to be

I'm friendly.
She's creative.
Are you shy?
I'm not very quiet.

Habits · Present Simple

I play games.
She plays volleyball.
Do you like music?
He doesn't play soccer.

Reasons · Because

I love art because it's fun.
I'm into sports because I like teamwork.

Stretch · A2 expressions

I'm good at drawing.
I'd like to travel.
I'd rather stay home.

Level check: A1 = give a short answer. A1+ = add one detail. A2 = explain why and ask a follow-up question.

MISSION 04 · MAIN CHALLENGE

The Choice Lab 🎮

MISSION 05 · BRANCHING CONVERSATION

Meet Jamie! 💬

Choose your response to a new classmate. The reaction changes based on your answer. Read the dialogue aloud and try a different choice.

MISSION 06 · COMMUNICATIVE PRACTICE

Two Truths and a Dream 🌟

Tell your teacher two true things about yourself and one dream for the future. The teacher asks questions and guesses which one is your dream.

Round 1 · About me

Say two facts about your life: “I like...” / “I can...” / “I usually...”

Round 2 · My dream

Say one thing you want to do: “I'd like to...” / “My dream is to...”

Round 3 · Ask back

Ask the teacher three questions about their interests or goals.

Level ladder:

A1: Say three simple sentences.
A1+: Add details and use because.
A2: Ask three follow-up questions and compare your answers.

MISSION 07 · CREATE & APPLY

My Personal Profile Card 🪪

Create a short introduction. Share only details you feel comfortable sharing. You can invent a nickname and skip personal information.

MISSION 08 · FINAL QUEST & REFLECTION

The 2-Minute Me Challenge 🏆

Introduce yourself without reading!

Tell us: who you are, three adjectives, two hobbies, one skill, and one goal. Ask your teacher two questions.

A1 challenge: 5 connected sentences.
A1+ challenge: Add two reasons using because.
A2 challenge: Speak for 2 minutes, ask follow-up questions, and react naturally.
